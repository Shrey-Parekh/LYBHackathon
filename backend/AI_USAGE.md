## Run-time AI in the product

Optical character recognition (Tesseract.js, in the driver's browser) reads the licence at the start of a shift, to check that the card is a driving licence. It does not decide anything else, and nothing it reads is kept. The ETA, delay risk, alerts and option ranking are deterministic rules (DESIGN.md section 6.9). If the optional "Where is my order?" assistant is built with a language model, record it here.
